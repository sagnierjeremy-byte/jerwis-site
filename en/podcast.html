● Season 02 · New · Ages 4-6

The 3 Little Lanterns

Alma, Lewis and James, pupils at a French school, discovered three magic lanterns up in the attic. In each episode, one of the three faces down a specific fear — and their lantern lights up at just the right moment. A resolved ending is mandatory, and it's built for the school run. Read the making-of →

Episode 01 · 12:28

Alma and the Monster Under the Bed

Alma is sure there's something moving under her bed at night. Her red lantern is going to show her what the grown-ups can't see anymore.

Voice cast
  • Paul K · Narrator ("bedtime storyteller")
  • Philippine · Alma (red lantern)
Episode 02 · 13:42

Lewis and the Fear of Falling

Lewis is learning to skate at the skatepark with his dad. Every time he stands up on the board, he already pictures himself on the ground. His blue lantern gives him exactly what he needs to stop staring at the floor.

Voice cast
  • Paul K · Narrator
  • Philippine · Lewis (blue lantern)
Episode 03 · 16:18

Lewis and James on the High Ropes Course

First time on a high ropes course for Lewis and James, two four-year-old cousins. James wants to do everything, Lewis sees the height and freezes. The lantern will pass from the brave one to the one who doubts — and not in the way you'd think.

Voice cast
  • Paul K · Narrator
  • Philippine · Lewis (blue lantern) + James (yellow lantern)
Episode 04 · 10:34

Lewis and James at the Basketball Tournament

First real tournament. A crowd, referees, an opposing team. James wants to shine, Lewis wants nobody looking at him. They're going to have to learn something neither of them saw coming: playing as a pair.

Voice cast
  • Paul K · Narrator
  • Philippine · Lewis (blue lantern) + James (yellow lantern)
