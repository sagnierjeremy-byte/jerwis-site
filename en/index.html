— 09 · Who's talking, and why

I do all this
first for me.

Hi. My name is Jérémy, I live in France, I'm an entrepreneur, father of a son, Kevin's twin brother (founder of Eurofiscalis). I spent fifteen years launching projects — work, personal — without ever writing a line of code. It never stopped me from moving forward.

You might be wondering why I share all this for free. I'll be completely transparent: this site exists first for me. The newsletters, I write them to force myself to do the watch every week — because without that constraint, I'd quit. The AI tools, I build them because I need them in my day-to-day as an entrepreneur. The path, I structured it because I'd have needed it myself, two years ago. Nobody wanted to hand it to me — so I'm laying it down here, for you.

Your guarantee

If a piece of content reaches you, it's because it served me first. Never the other way around. Promise.

That's my only promise. No "content for content's sake," no inflated SEO, no filler articles to fill an editorial calendar. When I take the time to write something, it's because it saved me time, money or ideas. And I have to tell you this too: I use Claude, GPT and Gemini to structure, write, fact-check. I don't hide it — that's exactly the point — and every edition goes through my own eyes before it ships.

The goal isn't for you to copy me. It's for you to get inspired and adapt it to your business, your day-to-day, your family. A question, a disagreement, a topic to dig into, a piece of negative feedback? Reply to the newsletter. It lands straight in my inbox, and I read everything, I reply to everything.
