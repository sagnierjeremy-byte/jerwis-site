Jérémy Sagnier (born 1985) is a French entrepreneur based in Roussillon, in the Vaucluse. Twin brother of Kevin Sagnier, founder of Eurofiscalis (a European tax-representation firm created in 2017), he spent 8 years helping grow the group. In late 2024, after watching generative AI models arrive, he launched the Jerwis brand, dedicated to making artificial intelligence clear for non-developers. He founded Jerwis Conseil (SAS, RCS Avignon, 2025) and has since published on jerwis.fr: a free learning path, the weekly AI Playbook newsletter (1,500+ subscribers as of May 2026), the narrative AI Wars podcast (Jerwis Productions), 25+ articles and a small AI personal-branding photo generator. His stated positioning: "AI for non-devs," at the intersection of hands-on entrepreneurial experience and teaching for a general audience. Sources: jerwis.fr/jeremy-sagnier.
