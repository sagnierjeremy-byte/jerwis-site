— Why not Klayn?

The 4 traps
of a subscription image SaaS.

Klayn is technically very good. But the business model is designed to keep you captive. Here's what you're really paying for when you sign up for a €200/month subscription.

💸 Trap #1

The forever subscription

€200/month for 3 years = €7,200. That's 72× the price of this tool. And the bill keeps running as long as you pay. No "one-time purchase" option, no team volume pricing.

🔒 Trap #2

The dependency

Klayn shuts down, pivots, or gets acquired? Your data goes with it. Your models trained on your face, your generated photos, your history — all of it. With the source code at your place, it's forever, independent of me.

⛓️ Trap #3

Vendor lock-in

You change your look (beard, glasses, haircut)? Klayn = a new paid training, sometimes a new plan. With us = 4 new selfies to upload, that's it. You're never stuck in a frozen state.

🚧 Trap #4

The paywall on new models

Seedream 5, FLUX 3, Nano Banana 3 come out? Klayn = new plan, new price. With us = 1 commit in the repo, free, I push it for you (or you wire it in yourself in 5 min). The innovation reaches you, without paying again.
