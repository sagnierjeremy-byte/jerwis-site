AI, in order. No developer required.

I'm not a developer. With AI, I built a sales tool for my wife, an assistant that sorts my emails, and this website. If you can write a message, you already know the essentials. Here's the rest, in three steps.

So what is AI, really?

Picture a very capable colleague, available at any hour, that you write to in plain language. You ask, it answers, you adjust. At first, it helps you write, summarize and check. In step 3, it also does the work on your computer. You write sentences. Never code.

And concretely, what do people like you do with it?

Before you dive in

Your real questions.
Straight answers.

Everyone asks the same ones. Let's settle them now, so you can start with a clear head.

Do I need to know how to code? +

No. You write sentences in plain language, AI does the rest. Even in step 3, you don't type a single line of code.

How much does it cost? +

Reading the path costs €0. Steps 1 and 2 work with the free versions of ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Le Chat. Step 3 needs Claude Pro or ChatGPT Plus, about $20 a month before tax, cancel anytime. To see if it's worth it, read free or paid AI.

Do I need to install anything? +

Not to start. Steps 1 and 2 happen in your browser. For step 3, you install the Claude app or the ChatGPT app on your Mac or PC, like any other app.

Could I break something? +

No. You give it one folder, and it works inside it. You see what it does and you can stop it at any time.

Will AI replace my job? +

What replaces you is someone who knows how to use it. This path is here so that someone is you.

Step 01 · Free, in your browser

Start
simple.

You open ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Le Chat, on the free plan. You learn to pick your tool, ask well and check the answer. Nothing to install.

4reads ~35 minin total LevelBeginner PrerequisitesNone
⚡ Do it now · 2 min

Your first real prompt, right now.

Before reading anything, open ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Le Chat (all free), paste this, and replace the orange part with what you want to understand.

The prompt 
You are a patient teacher who explains without jargon.
Explain simply [what you want to understand, for example GDPR, an electricity bill, what AI is].
Give me: 1) a one-sentence definition, 2) an everyday example, 3) the one thing to remember.
What you'll see

An answer in 3 clear points, no fluff. And above all, you just wrote a real prompt, with a role ("patient teacher"), a precise request and a set format. That's the whole craft. The rest of the path just refines it.

Get a real document summarized.

Grab one of your own PDFs (a contract, an invoice, meeting notes), drop it into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Le Chat, and paste this.

The prompt 
Summarize this document in 5 simple points.
Then tell me the 3 things I should watch out for, with the page where you found each one.
If a piece of information isn't in the document, say so instead of making it up.
What you'll see

A short summary with the pages cited. You check it in 10 seconds, as you learned in step 1. Don't upload confidential documents to a free plan.

Step 03 · In the Claude or ChatGPT app

Put it
to work
for you.

In the Claude app or the ChatGPT app, AI doesn't just answer anymore. It creates documents, spreadsheets and pages, in a folder on your computer. You still talk to it in plain language.

4reads ~38 minin total LevelBeginner ToolsClaude Pro or ChatGPT Plus
⚡ Do it now · 10 min

Your first real task in the app.

You need a Claude Pro or ChatGPT Plus plan. Then, four moves.

  1. Install the Claude app or the ChatGPT app, on Mac or Windows, and sign in.
  2. Create an empty folder called "ai-test" on your desktop.
  3. In Claude, give it that folder. In ChatGPT, switch to Work mode.
  4. Paste this message and replace the orange part.

Still seeing two buttons, "Chat" and "Cowork", in Claude? Pick Cowork.

The message 
In the ai-test folder, create a simple web page that presents my business: [your job and your city].
Explain what you're doing in simple words.
When it's ready, show me the result.
What you'll see

AI asks you a question or two, creates the page in your folder and shows it to you. You just put it to work on your computer, without a single line of code.
