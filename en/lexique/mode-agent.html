Agent mode

Agent mode is the difference between an AI that tells you what to do and one that does it. You give it a goal, it decides the steps, uses tools, checks its work and keeps going until it gets there.

Definition

Agent mode, in plain words

An answer is text you still have to act on. An action is the AI opening a page, filling a spreadsheet or tidying a folder for you.

It saves a lot of time, and it needs more care. The more the AI acts alone, the more you should review anything that cannot be undone, like a payment or a sent message.

A concrete example

You ask "find me three trains to Lyon on Friday morning". In agent mode, the AI opens the site, compares the times and suggests the three, instead of giving you tips on how to search.

Why it matters

It is what saves the most time, and what needs the most attention.

In Cowork on Claude, Work on ChatGPT, AI browsers like Comet or Atlas, and automation tools.

Don't mix it up with

Cowork (Claude) : Cowork is Claude working on your computer. You give it a folder, and it reads, sorts and creates files inside it.

ChatGPT Work : Work is the ChatGPT mode that completes a whole task, in several steps, and hands back a document, a spreadsheet, a deck or a site.

Common mistakes

  • Letting it pay or send a message without checking.
  • Giving it a vague instruction, so it heads the wrong way.
  • Thinking an agent never makes mistakes because it sounds confident.
