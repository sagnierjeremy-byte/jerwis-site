Imagen 4

Imagen 4 is Google DeepMind's image generator. You use it to produce clean visuals fast and cheap, especially if you already live in the Google ecosystem: it comes in three variants (Fast, Standard, Ultra) and it's built right into Gemini and Google Workspace.

— What it is

Imagen 4, in plain words

In plain words: you describe your image, and Imagen generates it for you, either in the Gemini app, or in Google Slides and Docs, or through the API for production.

Google put real care into two things I care about: prompt fidelity (it respects the details) and text inside the image (readable and well placed). It's become my plan B when Flux or Midjourney trip up on a specific case.

You get access in the Gemini app (the consumer side), in Google AI Studio to test it for free, and through Vertex AI for production.

What I use it for

The cases where Imagen 4 earns its keep for me:

  • Low-budget volume: generating 100 visuals in Fast mode costs $2. Perfect for testing at scale.
  • Visuals inside Google Slides or Docs: Imagen is built right into Workspace through Gemini. I type, I insert.
  • Text inside the image: posters and banners. It's been good since Imagen 3 already.
  • Ultra mode for final visuals: once I've locked in the concept, I rerun it in Ultra for the top-tier render.
  • SafeSearch-guaranteed visuals: for sensitive enterprise use, Google filters out forbidden content natively.

How it compares

vs Flux Pro: Flux is more precise on complex details. Imagen is cheaper and faster on volume.

vs Midjourney v7: Midjourney keeps the aesthetic edge. Imagen is more practical for production (official API, clear pricing).

vs DALL-E 3 (OpenAI): Pretty close call. DALL-E is built into ChatGPT, Imagen into Gemini. Pick based on your ecosystem.

What it costs

API pricing as of May 2026 (source: ai.google.dev/pricing):

  • Imagen 4 Fast: $0.02 per image
  • Imagen 4 Standard: $0.04 per image
  • Imagen 4 Ultra: $0.06 per image

On the consumer side: included in Google AI Pro at $19.99/month with a generous quota. Fully unlocked in Google AI Ultra at $249/month.

My take

Imagen 4 doesn't put out the prettiest images on the market. But at this price, on volume, it's unbeatable.

When I need a clean visual fast and I'm already in Gemini or Google Docs, I pull it up right there. Simpler than heading over to Midjourney for a single image.

If you live in the Google ecosystem, Imagen is your go-to reflex. Otherwise, Flux stays more versatile.

Quick questions

Does Imagen 4 have a public API?

Imagen 4 is Google DeepMind's image generation model in 2026, in three variants (Fast, Standard, Ultra). Available through the Gemini app, Google AI Studio, and Vertex AI, cheap and well integrated with Google Workspace.

Which variant should I choose?

Fast for testing at volume, Ultra for the final version.

What image sizes are possible?

1K and 2K output, several ratios (1:1, 16:9, 9:16, 4:3, 3:4).
