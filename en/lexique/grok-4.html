Model · xAI · 2025

Grok 4

Grok 4 is the 2025 version of the AI model from xAI, Elon Musk's company, built straight into X (the old Twitter) for Premium subscribers. Its unique edge: it reads X posts in real time, so it knows what's happening right now. It's the handy tool for keeping up with the news and the debates of the moment.

Newer versions exist. The most recent Grok is Grok 4.7. For today's models, see the up-to-date comparison.

— What it is

Grok 4, in plain words

In plain words: it's the AI plugged into X live. When a topic sets the network on fire, Grok gives you the context faster than scrolling back through your own feed.

Grok 4 is the version released in mid-2025. A 1-million-token context, real-time access to every X post (its unique edge), multimodal on the Heavy version, and solid benchmark scores. Its marketing pitch: "less filtered, funnier, ready to joke about anything."

Available through X (starting with the Premium plan at $8/month), through grok.com, and through an API for developers.

What it does for me

  • Real-time watch: Grok reads X posts live, which means it knows what's happening right now. Claude and GPT are usually about 6 months behind on the news.
  • Summarizing an X brawl: a thread with 500 replies, Grok boils it down to 5 points with each side's position.
  • Finding who came up with an idea: "who posted this first on X?" Grok tracks down the original source.
  • Testing a touchy subject: Grok has lighter moderation than Claude or ChatGPT. For journalistic research or a hard-hitting debate, that's useful.
  • Having fun: the tone is deliberately provocative. Works or doesn't, depending on your mood.

How it compares

vs GPT-5.5 / Claude Sonnet 4.6: Grok 4 is slightly behind on writing quality and reasoning, but it has a unique edge: direct, real-time access to X. Pick Grok for keeping up with X, the others for everything else.

vs Perplexity: Perplexity is better at general web search (sources cited cleanly). Grok is better on X specifically.

vs Gemini 3.1 Pro: Gemini is more accurate and cheaper. Grok has the built-in X ecosystem and a more direct tone.

What it costs

Prices as of May 25, 2026 (source: docs.x.ai):

  • API Input: $1.25 / million tokens
  • API Output: $2.50 / million tokens
  • X Premium: $8/month (Grok available with a reasonable limit)
  • X Premium+: $22/month (unlimited Grok + Grok Heavy)

My take

I use it almost exclusively to keep up with AI news on X. When some drama blows up (a Karpathy paper, an OpenAI announcement, a benchmark controversy), Grok gives me the context faster than my feeds do.

What I like: the real-time access to X, and a tone that's a change from the smoothed-over answers the others give.

What bugs me: the "provocative" tone sometimes feels forced, a bit teenage. And Elon Musk as the boss — not neutral, and it shows in the model's biases on certain political topics.

Quick questions

Is Grok really "unfiltered"?

Grok 4 is the 2025 version of xAI's AI model (Elon Musk's company), built into X for Premium subscribers. Newer versions exist, like Grok 4.7. Its unique edge is real-time access to X posts, handy for keeping up with the news.

Do you need to be on X to use Grok?

No, grok.com is open. But the X integration is the main advantage.

Is Grok open-source?

Grok 1 was released open-weight (2024). Grok 2, 3, and 4 are closed.
