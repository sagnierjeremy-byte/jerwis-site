Perplexity Comet

Perplexity Comet is Perplexity's web browser (built on Chromium, just like Chrome) with a built-in AI agent that can browse, click, and fill out forms for you. You tell it "find me 5 plumbers in Lyon with their reviews," and it does that while you get on with something else.

— What it is

Perplexity Comet, in plain words

In plain words: it's a real browser where an AI can take over the current tab to run searches and do tasks for you.

In practice: "book me this train ticket," "fill out this form with my info," "compare the 10 products in this tab." It takes over, browses, clicks, fills things in. I can stop it at any time.

Available on Mac, Windows, and Linux. On mobile, it's built into the Perplexity app on iOS and Android with a "Background Assistant" mode.

What I use it for

The cases where Comet saves me time:

  • Finding suppliers / providers: "find me 10 freelance designers in Paris with their portfolios and their rates." It does the rounds, and I get back a table.
  • Competitive research: "compare the prices of these 5 SaaS tools and their features." It goes through them and sums it up.
  • Repetitive form filling: sign-up forms, applications, and so on. I leave it running for 10 minutes and it's done.
  • Comparison shopping: "which of these 3 sites is cheapest for this product, shipping included?" It does the math.
  • Pulling info from several sites: it opens 10 tabs, reads them, and hands you a clean summary.

How it compares

vs ChatGPT Atlas / Operator (OpenAI): Atlas goes further with agents that really act (buy, confirm). Comet is faster at searching and summarizing. Atlas requires ChatGPT Pro at $200.

vs Arc Search / Dia (The Browser Company): Dia looks nicer on the UX and privacy side. Comet is more powerful in "do it for me" mode.

vs Chrome + an extension: Chrome is still more stable and compatible with everything. Comet brings a native agent that feels smoother than an extension.

How much it costs

Pricing as of May 2026 (source: perplexity.ai/comet):

  • Comet is free to download with the basic features
  • The advanced agent features need Perplexity Pro at $20/month
  • Perplexity Pro also includes GPT-5, Claude Sonnet 4.6, and premium search

So for $20/month you get Perplexity (AI search), Comet (the agent browser), and access to several big models at once. It's one of the best value-for-money deals on the market.

My take

I use it as a second browser for the tasks I want to delegate. My main browser is still Chrome, for ecosystem reasons (passwords, extensions).

That "magic" feeling the first time is real: you tell it to do something, you go grab a coffee, and it's done. On simple tasks, it's genuinely impressive. On complex ones (multiple steps, confirmations), it still trips up sometimes.

If you already pay for Perplexity Pro, download Comet — you'll use it. If you don't pay yet, it's a good reason to subscribe.

Quick questions

Does Comet replace Chrome?

Perplexity Comet is Perplexity's web browser, built on Chromium, with a built-in AI agent that can browse, click, and fill out forms for you. Free download, advanced agent features via Perplexity Pro.

Is my data private?

Perplexity says agent sessions aren't used to train its models by default. Worth checking in their privacy policy.

Can the agent make purchases for me?

Yes, but it asks for confirmation on payments. You can adjust that in the settings.
